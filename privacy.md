# Pomoville privacy policy

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

Pomoville is a focus app for iPhone made by **Pedro Pablo Soto Martínez**, an independent developer in Chile ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how you can delete it.

## Summary

- We don't ask for your name, email or location.
- No ads, and we don't sell data.
- Your village lives on your iPhone and is backed up to the cloud with an anonymous account.
- You can delete your account and your data from the app at any time.

## What data we use

| Data | Why | Where it's stored |
|---|---|---|
| **Your village**: game progress, focus sessions (length, time and optional tag), settings | To make the app work and back up your village | On your iPhone and in our cloud (Supabase, USA) |
| **Account identifier**: a random code the app creates on first use | To link your backup to your account | Supabase (USA) |
| **Device identifier** generated by the app (not Apple's advertising identifier) | To know which iPhone uploaded the latest copy | Supabase (USA) |
| **Sign in with Apple** (optional): the identifier Apple provides and the email you share, which may be Apple's private relay email | To restore your village on another iPhone | Supabase (USA) |
| **Pro subscription status**: whether you have Pro and when it renews | To unlock Pro features and restore purchases | RevenueCat (USA); Apple processes the payment |
| **Anonymous usage events**: for example "session finished", "build finished" or "new era", with game data (minutes, era, type of build) | To understand how the app is used and improve it | PostHog (USA) |
| **Crash reports**: what failed, the app version and the iPhone model | To fix unexpected crashes | Sentry (USA) |

Usage events and crash reports **do not include** your name, email, account id, IP address or anything you type (such as your tag or pet names). We don't use Apple's advertising identifier and **we don't track you** across other apps or websites.

Notifications are local: your iPhone schedules them and they never go through our servers.

## Who we share it with

Only with the providers that run the app, which process data on our behalf: **Supabase** (account and backup), **RevenueCat** (subscriptions), **PostHog** (anonymous analytics), **Sentry** (crash reports) and **Apple** (payments and Sign in with Apple). All of them store data in the United States. We don't sell or share data with anyone else.

## How long we keep it

- Your village and account: while you use the app or until you delete your account.
- Usage events: up to 1 year.
- Crash reports: up to 90 days.

## Your rights and how to delete your data

- **Delete everything:** in the app, *Settings → Account and Pro → Delete account and data*. Your account and your village are deleted from the cloud right away, Sign in with Apple access is revoked and the app starts over on your iPhone.
- **Take your data with you:** *Settings → Account and Pro → Export my village* creates a file with all your progress.
- To access, correct or object to the use of your data, or for any question, email **contacto.pomoville@gmail.com**.

## Children

Pomoville is suitable for all ages and doesn't ask for personal data to be used. If you believe a child has given us personal data (for example, by linking their Apple account), contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
